## The problem

Parts of Mesa, Arizona are poorly served by public transit. This graduate project at Arizona State University proposed new bus routes for underserved areas and modelled the emissions impact.

## Method

- **Spatial analysis:** QGIS analysis of census tracts to find transit gaps, with choropleth maps.
- **Route design:** five new bus routes with 45 stops, digitized along major arterials.
- **Accessibility:** 500m buffer zones around the new routes to model pedestrian access.
- **Impact:** CO₂ reductions modelled for diesel and electric buses under different ridership uptakes.
- **Communication:** maps, charts and carbon-equivalency comparisons to support the recommendations.

Built with QGIS, Python, GeoPandas and Matplotlib on public data (ACS, OpenStreetMap).
